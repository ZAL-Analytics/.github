# Overview

# ZAL Analytics

ZAL Analytics is a **research-driven, sovereignty-first** company building **air-gapped AI and data intelligence systems** for the institutions that carry Europe's hardest security responsibilities: **defense and military, law enforcement, and private security**. The analytical work these institutions depend on, and increasingly the AI that reasons over it, runs largely on proprietary platforms they cannot control, inspect, or operate without foreign infrastructure. We see that as a strategic risk and a governance gap we exist to close.

We develop an interconnected stack of analytical infrastructure, from data ingestion and entity resolution to visualization, reporting, and validation, and extend it into the layer that decides whether any of it can be trusted: **model integrity and provenance**. Every part is built to run **fully air-gapped**, on infrastructure the operator controls end to end, with no telemetry, no vendor connectivity, and no exposure to foreign legal reach.

As institutions adopt foundation models they did not build, the risk moves to the model itself: whether it has been tampered with, and whether its conclusions trace back to authorized sources. In defense, policing, and security operations, an unverifiable model is an operational liability. We treat the model, the evidence, and the chain between them as things that must be inspectable and provable, never taken on trust.

We distribute our stack through **free, open, and indefinite licenses granted exclusively to trusted institutions and vetted sponsors**: perpetual, source-available, and self-hosted, with no lock-in and no recurring dependency on us. Partners who qualify genuinely own what they run. They can read every line, audit every model, and operate indefinitely without our involvement. Access is deliberately gated to institutions whose mandate and governance we can trust. This is capability for the people responsible for European security, not a public commons.

Our mission is to replace dependence with capability: **sovereign, auditable, air-gapped** infrastructure that defense, law enforcement, and security institutions control end to end. Where not just the software but the models and the evidence behind every decision can be verified. We build openly with our partners, so what we deliver is durable beyond any single contract and reusable by the institutions we serve.

**European by design. Air-gapped by default. Sovereign by intent.**
