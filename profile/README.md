# ZAL Analytics

*A fully integrated, air-gapped AI intelligence stack, from hardware to applications.*

We build secure, locally controlled AI for public and private institutions in the EU/EEA that need AI and sensitive data to run without dependence on foreign cloud infrastructure. Our systems are Rust-native, explainable, and work on-premises, at the edge, or offline.

## The Stack

- **Portable AI machines**: battery-powered hardware for air-gapped AI, deployable anywhere.
- **Secure portal & data intelligence**: preconfigured for specific industries.
- **AI-integrated IDE**: secure development with AI built in.
- **Standalone AI tools**: usable on their own or as part of the stack.

## Core Technology

- **AI inference**: energy-efficient execution on embedded devices and phones.
- **Entity resolution**: explainable, GPU-accelerated probabilistic matching.
- **Knowledge graphs**: ontology-driven extraction with provenance on every fact.
- **Secure data movement**: immutable, read-only sync across controlled environments.
- **Licensing**: offline licensing with eIDAS timestamping.

## Why It Matters

Institutions adopting AI often have to choose between capability and control. We remove that trade-off with systems that run locally, stay inspectable, and support accountable decisions without network access.

> **Open source. Local by default. Air-gapped or hybrid.**
